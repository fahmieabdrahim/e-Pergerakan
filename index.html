Staff Movement & Attendance System

System Guide & Workflow

Overview

Digital system to monitor daily movements, attendance status, and activity approval workflow in real time.

Installation

  1. Open the link in your mobile browser (Chrome or Safari).
  2. Tap the browser menu and select Add to Home Screen for instant app-like access.

Guide & Workflow

  1. Setup: Tap Settings to create a new board, then add members with their position and role (Staff or Supervisor).
  2. Submit: Members tap their profile to submit movement requests (Leave, Meeting, Official Outing). Status starts as Pending.
  3. Approve: Supervisors receive notification badges to review, approve, or reject requests.
  4. Display: Approved movements update instantly across the planner, monthly calendar, and daily tabs (In Office, HBH, Others).
